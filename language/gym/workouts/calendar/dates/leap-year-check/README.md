# Workout: Gregorian Leap Year Check

Read a positive integer `year` from stdin. Print `true` if it is a Gregorian leap year, else `false`:
- Years divisible by `400` are leap years.
- Other years divisible by `100` are **not** leap years.
- Otherwise, years divisible by `4` are leap years.
- Non-positive years (`<= 0`) print `false`.
