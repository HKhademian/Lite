## Concept

Explores script `ℓ` (U+2113), calligraphic `ℒ` (U+2112), and typographic variations of `"l"` (`ł`, `ȴ`, `ɭ`) on the `16×16` monoline grid so each mark stands alone as **`$`** and replaces `L`/`l` in **`$ite`**.

## Candidates (`R8-01` – `R8-06`)

1. **`R8-01` · Script `ℓ` (`r8-01-script-ell.svg`)**: Classic `ℓ` in `mini`-grade `16×16` monoline—1 continuous loop flowing into `ite`.
2. **`R8-02` · Feather-Quill `ℓ` (`r8-02-feather-ell.svg`)**: Opens the top of `ℓ` into a feather vane with a single inner barb.
3. **`R8-03` · Script Capital `ℒ` (`r8-03-script-L-cap.svg`)**: Calligraphic `ℒ` in 1 continuous stroke—distinctive standalone `$` app icon.
4. **`R8-04` · Barred `ł`-Ray (`r8-04-barred-l-ray.svg`)**: Inspired by `ł`/`ƚ`—a clean `l` crossed by a single diagonal light-ray slash. Crisp at `16 px`.
5. **`R8-05` · Curled Hook `ȴ`/`ɭ` (`r8-05-curled-l-hook.svg`)**: Inspired by curled/retroflex `l`—1 continuous stroke ending in an upward wing curl.
6. **`R8-06` · Bolt-Loop `ℓ` (`r8-06-bolt-ell.svg`)**: Angular lightning-faceted `ℓ` combining `ℓ`'s silhouette with bolt sharpness.
