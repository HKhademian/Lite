Lite — single-mark concepts

Direction: one mark that is at once a lowercase l (the "mini m" trick — the logo is the letter), a feather/quill (lightness), and where possible a ray of light / lightning. All geometry is authored as minimal SVG paths; teal kept from the current VS Code icons (#0F766E / #5EEAD4).

C1 · Plume-l

quill + l · stroke

lite

Two strokes: a vane loop and a near-vertical spine that runs past the base as a nib flick. The spine is the stem of a lowercase l; the loop is the feather. Lightest of the set.

C2 · Plume solid

feather · filled + knocked-out spine

lite

Same plume as one filled path with the quill knocked out. Boldest presence at any size; most "brand-ready" for avatars and store tiles. Reads feather first, l second.

C3 · Bolt-l

lightning + l · stroke

lite

The light reading you like: the top third is a lightning kink, then it falls into the stem of l. One path, two meanings — lite and light. Most energetic; least feather-like.

C4 · Ray (current, refined)

l + light dot · stroke

lite

Evolution, not revolution: the current VS Code mark with the foot dropped so the stroke is a pure lowercase l, and the dot kept as a point of light beside it — "l·" as in lite + light. Closest to today's identity.

C5 · Tile (app icon)

C2 geometry, reversed on teal tile

How a chosen glyph behaves as a filled app tile / GitHub avatar — the C2 silhouette used as an outline on brand teal. Shows the polarity pattern, not a separate concept.
