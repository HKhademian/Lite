## What this round accounts for

Revision 3 (`rev-3-quill/notes.md`) established six hard constraints from testing ~40 marks down to `16 px`:
1. Solid blobby feathers read as leaves/arrows.
2. Vertical stem + multiple barbs reads as `K` or a fern.
3. Vertical stem + closed vane reads as `P`, `D`, or a flag (eliminating Rev 4's `a1-loop-quill`).
4. Two-sided symmetry kills lightness (reads as wheat).
5. Pure abstract slashes have no anchor.
6. Diagonal + monoline + one-sided survives—**and** Rev 3 explicitly requested a *filled, tapered-silhouette* next craft step so the quill cannot be mistaken for a stock icon, while holding **Spark** (`li`) open as the most `mini`-like `16 px` candidate.

Revision 5 puts the four strongest non-redundant contenders side-by-side on the same Paper/Ink editorial sheet:

1. **1 · Tapered Quill (`1-tapered-quill.svg`)**: Executes Rev 3's requested next craft step—a single calligraphic, tapered silhouette at 35° with one razor-clean notch and a needle nib, paired with a drawn `lite` wordmark whose `l` top terminal is sheared at 35°.
2. **2 · Slanted Quill-`l` (`2-slanted-quill-l.svg`)**: Solves the `mini` "first-letter monogram" problem without triggering Rev 3's Findings 2 & 3. By leaning the `l` forward (~18°) with a single upward feather flick, it never reads as `P`, `D`, or `K`, yet slots directly into the `lite` wordmark as the `l`.
3. **3 · Spark `li` (`3-spark-li.svg`)**: Refines Rev 3's held-open **Spark** direction. A pure vertical `l` paired with a crisp 4-point Filament (`#F0B45A`) spark. In the `lite` wordmark, that spark *is* the dot of the `i`. Zero optical-cut needed at `16 px`.
4. **4 · Split-Light Quill (`4-split-light-quill.svg`)**: Unifies **Feather + Light + Lightning** into one `16×16` monoline mark—a 35° diagonal shaft with a single crisp lightning-step barb.
