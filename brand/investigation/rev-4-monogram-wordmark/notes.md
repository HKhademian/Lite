## Purpose of Revision 4

In `MiniLang/brand/`, the mark (`mini-mark.svg`) is not a separate illustration placed next to the wordmark—it **is** the first letter `m` of `mini-wordmark.svg`, drawn on a 16×16 monoline grid (`stroke-width="2"`, round caps and joins), colored in the brand accent while `ini` stays in the text color.

Revision 3 (`rev-3-quill/`) explored standalone 64×64 diagonal quill drawings (`lite-mark.svg` and `lite-mark-16.svg`). Revision 4 tests the complementary question: **what if Lite's mark uses the exact same 16×16 monoline architecture as `mini`, where the monogram itself acts as the `l` (or `L`) in `lite`?**

## Candidates in this folder

- `a1-loop-quill.svg` — **The Loop Quill `l`**: 1 continuous path rising up the stem, arcing into a feather vane, and tucking back into the spine.
- `a2-single-barb-plume.svg` — **The Single-Barb Plume `l`**: upright `l` stem with a single swept feather barb and top plume curve.
- `a3-swept-rachis.svg` — **The Swept Quill `l`**: gently bowed feather shaft (rachis) with one clean upward vane stroke.
- `b1-photon-ray.svg` — **The Photon `l` & `i` Bridge**: vertical stem + 45° light ray whose photon dot bridges directly into the dot of the `i` in `lite`.
- `b2-refracted-beam.svg` — **The Refracted Beam `l`**: vertical stem + single 45° upward ray/barb (reads as both a refracted light ray and a minimal 1-barb feather).
- `c1-bolt-l.svg` — **The Bolt `l` (Ignite)**: 1 continuous 4-point zig-zag `l` stroke.
