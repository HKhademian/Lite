# Lite — mark exploration, revision 7 (`$` Letter Marks for `$` & `$ite`)

Status: **working exploration, 2026-11-12**.
Previous revisions: `../rev-1-concepts/` through `../rev-5-synthesis/`, plus Rev 6 in `../studio.html`.

## Goal

Explore single-letter `$` marks that clearly read as `L` or `l` so Lite can use the exact same brand mechanism as `mini`:
- Standalone icon = `$`
- Wordmark = `$ite` (replacing `L`/`l` automatically)

## Candidates (`R7-01` – `R7-08`)

1. **`R7-01` · Multi-Barb Quill `L` (`r7-01-quill-branches-l.svg`)**: Heavy `L` spine + foot (`stroke-width="2"`) with 3 lighter upward feather branches (`stroke-width="1.35"`), keeping `L` dominant.
2. **`R7-02` · Swept Quill-Foot `L` (`r7-02-swept-quill-foot-l.svg`)**: 35° leaning quill shaft that lands in a crisp horizontal `L` foot, with 3 spaced feather branches.
3. **`R7-03` · Lightning Bolt `L` (`r7-03-bolt-L.svg`)**: 1 continuous stroke—lightning kink on the stem landing in a sharp `L` foot.
4. **`R7-04` · Minimal Bolt `l` (`r7-04-bolt-l-minimal.svg`)**: Ultra-minimal 1-stroke lowercase `l` with a tight lightning zig-zag.
5. **`R7-05` · Spark `Li` (`r7-05-spark-Li.svg`)**: Rounded `L` + 4-point spark that doubles as the `i` dot in `Lite`/`lite`.
6. **`R7-06` · Feather-Foot `L` (`r7-06-feather-foot-L.svg`)**: 1 continuous stroke `L` whose horizontal foot sweeps upward like a single feather plume.
7. **`R7-07` · Light-Beam `L` (`r7-07-light-beam-L.svg`)**: Crisp `L` whose corner emits a 45° light ray upward.
8. **`R7-08` · Wing-Tip `l` (`r7-08-wing-tip-l.svg`)**: 1 continuous stroke—a minimal `l` whose top terminal flares into a light feather tip and curves into a subtle foot.
